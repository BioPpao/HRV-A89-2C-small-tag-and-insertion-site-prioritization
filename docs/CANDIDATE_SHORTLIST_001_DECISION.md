## 1. Purpose

This document defines the decision framework between the completed conservation/structural screening phase and the future tag × site perturbation modeling phase.

The objective is not to declare a validated insertion position. The objective is to define a transparent candidate set for further computational comparison.

No candidate is considered safe, validated, or experimentally proven.

---

## 2. Evidence hierarchy

Candidate evaluation follows:

1. Direct functional constraints and experimentally supported motifs
2. Structural compatibility from audited monomer and hexamer models
3. Evolutionary conservation and indel-context evidence
4. Literature-rescue evidence from homologous systems
5. Future tag-specific perturbation modeling

Lower-level evidence cannot override higher-level functional conflicts.

---

## 3. Candidate selection principle

A junction should be promoted only when:

- no dominant hard functional exclusion exists;
- structural exposure and geometry are acceptable;
- evolutionary evidence does not indicate extreme constraint;
- unresolved risks are explicitly recorded.

A candidate can remain useful even with uncertainty if it represents an important comparison point.
