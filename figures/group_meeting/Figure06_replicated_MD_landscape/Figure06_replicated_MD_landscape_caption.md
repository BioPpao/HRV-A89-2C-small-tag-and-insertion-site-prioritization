**Fig. 6 | Replicated MD identifies persistent nonlocal tag contacts as the most discriminating dynamic readout.** **a,** Design and workload. Broad screening comprised WT and 12 tagged systems with three independent 20-ns replicas per system (39 trajectories; 780 ns). Independent corrected CHARMM36 protocol validation comprised WT and five tagged systems with the same replication (18 trajectories; 360 ns). The combined 57 trajectories represent 1.14 microseconds of cumulative screening/validation sampling, not one converged simulation. **b,** Task010 corrected, PBC-aware broad reanalysis. Small translucent points show replicas and larger outlined points show construct means for WT-reference ensemble RMSD, junction-matched local RMSF relative to WT, WT-defined contact retention and persistent nonlocal tag-contact fraction. Rows follow scientific role rather than an MD composite score. **c,** Replica-level tag behavior in corrected-protocol validation. Persistent nonlocal tag-contact fraction is plotted against exposed tag-residue fraction for all 15 tagged trajectories. The 289|290 x MAP8 candidate hypothesis retains low persistent nonlocal contact; 248|249 x HA is replica heterogeneous; 256|257 x MAP8 is MD-neutral-like for this readout but remains biologically conflicted; and 224|225 x MAP8 and hard-negative 155|156 x MAP8 reproduce high-contact cautions. Directional annotations define no favorable quadrant or threshold. **d,** Task009 corrected reanalysis and independent corrected-protocol validation shown side-by-side for five retested constructs. Dots show three replicas per dataset, open diamonds show dataset-specific means and lines indicate mean direction only. The datasets are independent 3 x 20-ns ensembles; they were not pooled as six replicas or treated as paired trajectories. Persistent nonlocal tag-contact fraction gives the clearest candidate-control separation, and independent validation reproduces the main pattern. Three 20-ns replicas are adequate for screening, not mechanistic convergence. MD remains downstream comparative evidence and does not override direct homolog phenotype, hard functional constraints or absent direct HRV-A89 phenotype.
